Meet the PDCU AI Assistant

Ask a question the way you would ask a teller. The assistant answers from People Driven Credit Union’s own website content, links to the page it used, and points you to the right next step: apply online, log in to Online Banking, or book a branch appointment.

Try asking

  • “What are today’s auto loan rates?”
  • “Am I eligible to join PDCU?”
  • “How do I open a 9-month CD?”
  • “I lost my debit card, what do I do?”
  • “What are the Livonia branch hours?”
  • “How does PD Quick Cash work?”

Good to know

  • Answers come only from PDCU’s published content, with links to the source pages.
  • The assistant never asks for, and cannot look up, account numbers, Social Security numbers or balances. For anything account-specific it sends you to Online Banking or Member Services.
  • Loan questions end with a one-click path to apply or to reach a loan officer.

People Driven Credit Union is a State-Wide Michigan credit union offering credit cards, home equity loans, mortgages, personal loans, checking accounts, savings accounts, certificates of deposit, online and mobile banking, and much more. Manage your finances online or visit us at one of our Michigan branches in Livonia, Southfield, Warren, Ypsilanti, and Romeo.

NCUA & Equal Housing Statements: People Driven Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration. We are an Equal Housing Lender. Your savings are federally insured up to $250,000 and backed by the full faith and credit of the United States Government.

SAFE Act Disclosure: NMLS #776727. For mortgage-related services, contact a registered Mortgage Loan Originator (MLO). NMLS identifiers for MLOs and the Credit Union are available upon request, at the time of application, and included on all mortgage-related disclosures. For more information, visit NMLS Consumer Access.
